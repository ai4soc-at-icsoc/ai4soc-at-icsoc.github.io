# Program

**SOC4AI 2026** will take place on **Tuesday, 1 December 2026, 16:00–17:30 (CET)** in the **Major room**, Lodz University of Technology (Politechnika Łódzka), Campus B, ul. Wólczańska 217, 90-451 Łódź, Poland. See the [ICSOC 2026 program overview](https://icsoc2026.it.p.lodz.pl/program-overview.html) and the [venue information](https://icsoc2026.it.p.lodz.pl/conference-venue.html).

The session follows the two perspectives of the workshop: it opens with two papers that apply **AI to service-oriented systems** (composition and root cause localization) and continues with two papers that use **service-oriented principles to engineer AI applications** (governed LLM routing and an LLM-based SOA system for healthcare).

Each paper has a **20-minute slot: 15 minutes of presentation and 5 minutes of Q&A**.
