# Previous Editions

## SOC4AI 2025

**2nd International Workshop on Service-Oriented Computing for AI Applications**  
Co-located with [ICSOC 2025](https://icsoc2025.hit.edu.cn/), Shenzhen, China.  
Submissions: **10**. Accepted papers: **5**. Publication: LNCS post-proceedings.

### Organizers

- José Antonio Parejo Maestre, SCORE Lab, I3US Institute, Universidad de Sevilla, Spain.
- José María García, SCORE Lab, I3US Institute, Universidad de Sevilla, Spain.
- Víctor Casamayor Pujol, Distributed Intelligence and Systems-Engineering Lab, Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Spain.

### Program

SOC4AI 2025 was scheduled on **1 December 2025**, 14:00-15:30, at **China Merchants Hall 2**. The session chairs were José Antonio Parejo Maestre and José María García.

### Accepted Papers

- Valeria Maria Fortina and Pierluigi Plebani. **An LLM-Based Approach to Generating Authorization Policies for Data Products**.
- Manuel Otero Barbasán, Javier Fernández-Castillo, José María García and Pablo Fernandez. **Enhancing Microservice Observability Through AI Agents in Decentralized Telemetry**.
- Kenji Matsumoto and Yohei Murakami. **Guided Reasoning Chains for API Recommendation**.
- Jun Huang and Yan Liu. **PROV-MLXOps: Towards Provenance-Based Machine Learning and Explainability Operations**.
- Zihang Wang, Boris Sedlak and Schahram Dustdar. **Active Inference-Based Adaptive Routing for Heterogeneous Edge AI Services**.

External references: [ICSOC 2025 workshop listing](https://icsoc2025.hit.edu.cn/20002/list.htm), [ICSOC 2025 workshop program](https://icsoc2025.hit.edu.cn/19955/list.htm), [Springer ICSOC 2025 Workshops proceedings](https://link.springer.com/book/9789819217656).
