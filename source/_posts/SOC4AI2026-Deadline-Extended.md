**The SOC4AI 2026 paper submission deadline has been extended to 15 October 2026 at 23:59 AOE (Anywhere on Earth, GMT - 12). This is the final deadline.**

The ICSOC 2026 Workshop Chairs announced the extension on 22 September 2026, establishing a common final submission deadline for all ICSOC 2026 workshops.

Submit your paper through the [ICSOC 2026 EasyChair submission system](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=icsoc2026), selecting the **SOC4AI 2026 workshop track**.

Please see the [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/), [submission instructions](/submission/), and [important dates](/important-dates/) for details.
