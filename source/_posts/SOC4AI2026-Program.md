**The SOC4AI 2026 program is now available.** The workshop will take place on **Tuesday, 1 December 2026, 16:00–17:30**, in the **Major room** of Lodz University of Technology (Campus B), as part of ICSOC 2026.

Four papers will be presented, covering LLM-driven service composition, causal root cause localization, governed LLM routing, and an LLM-based SOA system for cardiovascular data processing.

See the full [program](/program/) for the schedule and presentation details.
